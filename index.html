What is KnowAI

KnowAI is a straightforward guide to understanding and using artificial intelligence responsibly. It helps people see what AI really is, what it isn’t, and how to use it effectively without losing sight of human judgment.

KnowAI breaks down the basics of AI and machine learning in simple terms, clears up common misconceptions, and introduces a variety of useful tools— because there’s a lot more out there than just ChatGPT.

The goal is to make AI easier to understand, more practical, and ultimately more ethical.

We believe the choice is simple: Know AI, or no AI.
